# ⚓ The ANCHOR Framework

### Nail any interview by answering from truth, not performance.

[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](LICENSE)

> An anchor doesn't make a ship look impressive. It keeps it steady when the water moves.
>
> This framework is for staying grounded in what's true about you — so the pressure of an interview room can't knock you off it.

---

## Why this exists

Most interview advice teaches you to **perform** — to package yourself, rehearse lines, and project confidence you may not feel.

This framework does the opposite. It's built on one belief, proven over and over in real interviews:

> **The precise truth, delivered with structure, beats the polished exaggeration every time.**

It was written by someone going through a layoff and a job search, who realized the heaviest thing he was carrying into interviews had nothing to do with his skills.

📖 **The complete framework — with examples, the preparation ritual, and the mindset section — is in [ANCHOR-FRAMEWORK.md](ANCHOR-FRAMEWORK.md).**

## Meet Muse — your interview companion

This repo ships with a persona file ([`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md)) that turns [Claude Code](https://docs.claude.com/claude-code) — or any AI assistant you paste it into — into **Muse**: a grounded, honest interview and career coach that uses the ANCHOR framework.

Muse won't help you inflate yourself. Muse helps you find the *true* version of your answer and tell it with structure and confidence. It asks the question underneath your question. It holds up a mirror with real evidence. It meets you as a person, not a résumé.

**To use it:**
1. Open this repo in Claude Code (`claude` from the repo root), or
2. Paste the contents of [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md) into any AI chat as a system prompt / first message
3. Tell Muse the role you're targeting and the question you're stuck on
4. Answer honestly — Muse builds from your truth, never invents it

## How to use this framework

- **Learning it:** Read the Values until you believe them. Practice the ANCHOR method on five common questions until the structure feels *natural*, not memorized.
- **Before an interview:** Run the Preparation Ritual (in the full framework). Re-read the Values and the Mindset the morning of.
- **During:** Don't think about the framework. You prepared with it; now just have the conversation. The structure will be there underneath.
- **After:** Reflect. What landed? What didn't? What would you do differently? The framework improves every time you use it — just like you do.

## The one thing to remember

If you forget everything else:

> **Interview from truth, not performance.**

The most compelling version of you in any room is not an inflated version — it's the real one, told clearly, with the evidence to back it and the steadiness to own both your strengths and your edges.

You don't have to be more than you are. You have to be *precisely, confidently, what you actually are.*

That's enough. It was always enough.
